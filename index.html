A bit about me

I like knowing
why it broke.

That curiosity is what pulled me from building pages into backends, security, and the servers running them.

I’m a self-taught developer from Nepal. I learn by building, breaking things in my own lab, and following the problem until I understand it. I care about the small details: who can access a route, what happens when a service goes down, and whether someone else can read the code.

I’m still going deeper into system design and DevOps. I bring that same habit of asking questions to client work, along with clear updates and practical decisions.
