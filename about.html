About Sagar

Curiosity got me started.
Building keeps me here.

I’m a full-stack developer based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I like taking an idea apart, understanding what it needs, and putting it back together as something people can use.

Kathmandu, Nepal · UTC +05:45

I learned by making things.

An online store taught me that a product is much more than its pages. There are permissions to get right, data to model, and decisions that only show their consequences once someone actually uses the app.

Since then, I’ve worked on community platforms, chat and video features, and Wix plugins. My own projects, Janasagar and MCommunity, give me room to work through those problems across the whole stack.

Security changed how I build.

Hands-on practice with web vulnerabilities made me pay closer attention to everyday code: a route missing an ownership check, an upload that trusts its filename, or a session that stays valid after logout. I like finding those problems early and understanding the fix.

I’m honest about where I am.

I’ve studied MERN development and ethical hacking, and I’m continuing my Bachelor of Information Technology at Tribhuvan University. Much of my learning happens independently, through documentation, experiments, and debugging my own projects.

I’m growing into DevOps and system design. I won’t pretend to have every answer, but I’ll ask the questions, explain the tradeoffs, and keep you involved in the work.
